SAT

The digital SAT is adaptive: how you do in the first module decides how hard the second one gets. Preparing for it is different from preparing for the paper exam, and most material online still has not caught up.

One thing worth knowing. A lot of SAT material still refers to a “verbal” section and to an essay. Both belong to the old paper exam. The current test has Reading and Writing, and no essay at all — if a course you are looking at still says otherwise, it has not been updated since 2023.
