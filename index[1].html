How practice works here

Every answer comes with its reason

A score on its own teaches nothing. The point of a practice test is what you learn in the ten minutes after it.

Marked the moment you finish

No waiting and no answer sheet to cross-check. Every question is marked in place, right where you answered it.

The passage points back at itself

Each explanation links to the paragraph the answer came from, and the text lights up. Finding the sentence is the skill being tested.

Scores you can compare

Raw scores convert to the band scale used by the exam, so a test in August means the same thing as a test in November.
