IELTS Writing Task 2 · marking Academic

Nowadays, a lot of people thinks that government must spend more money on public transport instead of building new roads. In my opinion, I agree with this statement because of two main reasons which I will discuss below.

Firstly, public transport is very good for the environment. Cars produce a lot of pollution and this is bad for our planet. If more people use buses and trains, the air will be more cleaner and cities will be less noisy. For example, in my country the government builds a new metro line last year and now the traffic is not so bad.

GRASubject–verb agreement. people thinks → people think. In your opening sentence, this pulls GRA down straight away.
CCYour opinion, stated twice. "In my opinion" and "I agree" do the same job — keep one.
CCMechanical signposting. The examiner reads a memorised template. Name the two reasons instead.
LRLow-range vocabulary. very good for → beneficial to / far less damaging to. Phrasing like this caps LR at 6.0.
GRADouble comparative. more cleaner → cleaner.
GRATense doesn't match "last year". builds → built.

Questions

What people usually ask

How accurate is the AI score?
It usually lands within half a band of a teacher's mark. We won't promise an exact match — no tool can guarantee that, and anyone promising it isn't being straight with you. The score exists to show direction and specific mistakes, not to hand down a verdict.
Which criteria is my work marked against?
The official descriptors. For Writing: Task Response, Coherence and Cohesion, Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range and Accuracy. For Speaking: Fluency and Coherence, Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range and Accuracy, Pronunciation.
How is pronunciation assessed?
Separately from the words. Your recording is broken down sound by sound, so you see which phonemes are letting you down — not a vague "work on your pronunciation". Speech rate, pause length and count, and filler words are measured too.
Can I use the marking without taking classes?
Yes. Marking works on its own. Classes plug in whenever you want them, and not before.
