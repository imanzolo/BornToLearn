About Born to Learn

An online academy for English, IELTS and SAT, run from Almaty for students who need a score to get somewhere.

What we are trying to do

Two things, and they only work together. Practice that is available at any hour, marks itself, and explains its reasoning — and lessons with a teacher who can see the habit behind the mistake.

Software is very good at telling you that a sentence is wrong. It is not good at noticing that you have avoided the same structure forty times because you are not confident with it. That is the part a person does.

Where the material comes from

Every passage, question and prompt on this site is written here. Nothing is copied or reworded from a published textbook, which matters for two reasons: it is other people’s property, and reused material is material your competition has already memorised.

It also means the bank keeps growing. New tests go up every week rather than being fixed at whatever a book contained.

A note on the AI marking. Automatic scoring usually lands within about half a band of a teacher’s mark.
