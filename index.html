How practice works here

Every answer comes with its reason

A score on its own teaches nothing. The point of a practice test is what you learn in the ten minutes after it.

Marked the moment you finish

No waiting and no answer sheet to cross-check. Every question is marked in place, right where you answered it.

The passage points back at itself

Each explanation links to the paragraph the answer came from, and the text lights up. Finding the sentence is the skill being tested.

Scores you can compare

Raw scores convert to the band scale used by the exam, so a test in August means the same thing as a test in November.

The four criteria

TR · CC · LR · GRA

Writing is marked on Task Response, Coherence and Cohesion, Lexical Resource, and Grammatical Range and Accuracy. Your band is the average of the four, rounded to the nearest half. Most people lose their marks in the same two every time — which is useful, because it means the fix is narrow.

About Born to Learn

An online academy for English, IELTS and SAT, run from Almaty for students who need a score to get somewhere.

What we are trying to do

Two things, and they only work together. Practice that is available at any hour, marks itself, and explains its reasoning — and lessons with a teacher who can see the habit behind the mistake.

Software is very good at telling you that a sentence is wrong. It is not good at noticing that you have avoided the same structure forty times because you are not confident with it. That is the part a person does.

Where the material comes from

Every passage, question and prompt on this site is written here. Nothing is copied or reworded from a published textbook, which matters for two reasons: it is other people’s property, and reused material is material your competition has already memorised.

It also means the bank keeps growing. New tests go up every week rather than being fixed at whatever a book contained.
