The four criteria

TR · CC · LR · GRA

Writing is marked on Task Response, Coherence and Cohesion, Lexical Resource, and Grammatical Range and Accuracy. Your band is the average of the four, rounded to the nearest half. Most people lose their marks in the same two every time — which is useful, because it means the fix is narrow.
